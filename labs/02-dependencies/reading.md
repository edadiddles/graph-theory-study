# Milestone 2 reading and mathematical work

Read Diestel, *Graph Theory*, sixth edition, Chapter 1 in full, including examples,
notes, and its supplied proofs. The list below follows the
[published contents](https://diestel-graph-theory.com/Contents.pdf).

For each section, record proof identifiers and progress in `notes.md`. Try to
reconstruct supplied arguments, then review them against the text. Record gaps
and whether assistance was needed. Reading a proof and independently proving
a result are different accomplishments; describe your work accurately.

Choose selected exercises from the actual book and record their numbers and
expected learning purpose before attempting them. No exercise numbers or proof
identifiers have been invented here. Select them with the agent at a checkpoint
if you want help matching difficulty. Never use solutions as the selection guide.
